# MICROcosm: a protocol for a global microcosm experiment
Here we have the codes and data from the experiment coordinated by Dr. Gustavo Romero (UNICAMP, Brazil) and Dr. Martin Gossner (Swiss Federal Institute for Forest, Switzerland). 

We would like to inform you that the data and codes used in our project are currently being prepared for publication. Until this occurs, they will not be available for external use.

We appreciate your understanding and patience as we work to make these resources available. Once they are ready, we will make an official announcement and share the details for access.
